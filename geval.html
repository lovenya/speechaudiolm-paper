What the judge receives

Context-aware criteria receive the input and generated audio clips. Output-only criteria receive only the generated clip. Dataset captions and transcripts are not supplied to this v6 plausibility judge.

What the numbers mean

The v6 final score is the equally weighted mean of the five criterion ratings. These are model judgments, not human ratings. Full-test results report successful-score coverage for each system.
