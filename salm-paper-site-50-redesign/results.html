Evaluation

Full test-set results

Direct SpeechAudioLM systems and text-mediated cascades, evaluated on the complete clean-v2 test split for each direction. Coverage is printed beside every value.

Scope: aggregate results use all 1,878 Speech→Audio test pairs and all 2,197 Audio→Speech test pairs. Corpus metrics are never recomputed on the 50 website examples.

Speech → Audio

The model receives speech audio and generates an environmental soundscape. Lower is better for FAD, KAD, and KL PaSST; higher is better for all other metrics.

Audio → Speech

The model receives environmental audio and generates a plausible spoken utterance. Higher is better for all metrics shown.

Reading these results

Corpus-level metrics

FAD, KAD, Inception Score, and KL PaSST compare distributions. They produce one number for a complete corpus and therefore do not appear as individual-example scores.

Sample-level metrics

G-Eval Audio, UTMOS, DNSMOS, and target-caption CLAPScore produce per-example measurements that can be averaged. Individual values are available in the example explorer.
