Abstract

SpeechAudioLM studies two generative tasks: producing contextually plausible speech from an environmental audio scene, and producing a plausible environmental soundscape from spoken language. The work contributes direction-specific paired datasets, a listening-capable G-Eval Audio metric with criterion-level explanations, and direct bidirectional generation systems evaluated against text-mediated cascades.
