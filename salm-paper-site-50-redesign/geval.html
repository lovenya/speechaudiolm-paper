Primary metric contribution

G-Eval Audio

A listening-capable evaluator for end-to-end cross-modal audio generation. It reports a transparent 1–5 score built from separately judged criteria, with written feedback and exact prompt traces.

Aggregate G-Eval Audio v6

Reference-free plausibility results on the full direction-specific clean-v2 test sets.

What is judged

Transparent judgment trail

1. Listen independently

The judge receives the input context and generated output as ordered audio clips. Text captions and transcripts are not supplied to the v6 plausibility judge.

2. Score one criterion at a time

Each criterion receives its own rubric, feedback, and integer score from 1 to 5. The final score is their equally weighted mean.

3. Preserve feedback

The website exposes the criterion score and written explanation for every displayed example.

4. Preserve the prompt

The exact rendered v6 prompt—not a paraphrase—is available underneath each criterion judgment.
